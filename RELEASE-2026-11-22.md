walkthrough from the carrier activation screen to a custom recovery and optional
modem unlock.

## Three working recoveries

All are `vendor_boot_a` images built from the **12.1** core with the same device tree
and fixes. They support FT8725 touch, FBE `/data` decryption, backup/restore, MTP/ADB,
haptics and six 1080×2388 panels.

| Recovery | Why pick it | Size | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **OrangeFox-R12.0-vegas26.img** | Most features, `fox` CLI, ADB sideload verified; newer 12.1 core with thermal/bzip2/vibration/reboot-recovery fixes | 55.2 MB | `873b5614bbb5e378c972f7c78962eab658f5e55a6f3002bac6bc968597b6b5fc` |
| **TWRP-3.7.1-12-vegas26.img** | Plain familiar TWRP, smallest | 52.8 MB | `0a241558f40d4cd18b0b2299d0018ac0154362045691b329c8bfb4a6d562c5d7` |
| **PBRP-4.x-vegas26.img** | TWRP-based with a different UI | 54.9 MB | `c899f61ee38ad14ecccd352fa06fcf9e61737698487eb83001ec4a3d95d977cb` |

Tested 2026-11-22 with an encrypted `/data` and PIN `1234`: **17/17 on each** (UI,
touch, decrypt, `/sdcard` read/write, gatekeeper, haptics, MTP/ADB, six panels,
`/metadata`, backup dirs). **ADB sideload verified on OrangeFox 12.1.**

```powershell
fastboot flash vendor_boot_a OrangeFox-R12.0-vegas26.img
```

Then **Power + Volume Down → Volume Down to Recovery mode → Power**.
`fastboot reboot recovery` boots Android on this unit. Revert with
`stock-vendor_boot_a.img`.

## Full path, not just recovery

The docs now cover the whole setup:

1. Stop updates and confirm the `XT2617V` / `-4-3` build.
2. Bypass the Walmart/Straight Talk activation screen with Google's Test DPC QR, then
   remove the device-owner role.
3. DFRoot + KernelSU temporary root, then raw backups.
4. val-protocol LK patch, `frp` OEM-unlock byte, `fastboot oem unlock`.
5. Persistent Magisk on `init_boot_a` via `dd` (the unit rejects the fastboot write).
6. LineageOS 23.2 microG GSI: vbmeta, fastbootd `system`, then **stock recovery
   factory reset** — never `fastboot -w` for the first wipe.
7. Flash one of the three recoveries to `vendor_boot_a`.
8. Optional `vegas-freedom` modem patch, **`md1img_a` only**, with a non-Tracfone SIM
   to verify.

## GSI

`LineageOS-23.2-2026.11.18-microG-gsi.img.xz` — 1.09 GB compressed, 2.9 GB
decompressed, SHA-256 of the `.img`
`dcefcb1920cd538a80d4a0120996278f0a8a33aacb7c0ee429c50e61f1818345`. It is the tested
Android 16 microG build and includes `com.mediatek.ims` for VoLTE. There is no newer
Android 16 build as of 2026-11-22.

## Modem caveat

The patched `md1img_a` was flashed to this unit, but there was **no SIM available**, so
**network unlock is not verified**. The release does **not** contain a modem image:
`md1img` is device-unique. The flash kit includes the `vegas-freedom` tool and
instructions to patch **your own** stock image. Flash `md1img_a` only; never
`md1img_b`, `lk`, `preloader`, `seccfg`, `gpt`, `nvram`, `nvdata`, `protect*` or
`persist`.

A network unlock and a carrier's IMEI activation policy are separate; a Verizon MVNO
may still reject the IMEI. See [`docs/07-modem-unlock.md`](https://github.com/Deep865/moto-g-power-2026-xt2617v-walmart-unlock-degoogle-guide/blob/main/docs/07-modem-unlock.md).

## Not usable on this phone

- **OrangeFox 14.1 / 16.0**: build, but no usable recovery UI on this Android 16
  vendor; full images can exceed the ~55.5 MB `vendor_boot` content limit.
- **TWRP 14.1**: same userspace blocker.

We backport useful fixes from those branches into 12.1 only after the 17/17 test. See
[`docs/13-backport-roadmap.md`](https://github.com/Deep865/moto-g-power-2026-xt2617v-walmart-unlock-degoogle-guide/blob/main/docs/13-backport-roadmap.md).
