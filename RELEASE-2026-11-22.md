# Release 2026-11-22 — three working recoveries for the Moto G Power 2026 (XT2617V)

Walmart / Straight Talk `XT2617V`, codename `vegas26`, MT6835. All three images are
**`vendor_boot` for slot A** and are built from the **12.1** core with the same device
tree and fixes.

## Pick one

| Recovery | Why | Size |
|---|---|---:|
| **OrangeFox-R12.0-vegas26.img** | Most features, MTK/Trustonic friendly, `fox` CLI; ADB sideload verified | 55.1 MB |
| **TWRP-3.7.1-12-vegas26.img** | Plain TWRP, the familiar UI; smallest | 52.8 MB |
| **PBRP-4.x-vegas26.img** | TWRP-based, different colourful UI | 54.9 MB |

All three support Focaltech FT8725 touch, FBE `/data` decryption, backup/restore,
MTP/ADB, haptics and the six 1080×2388 panels.

## Verified

- 12.1 core: **17/17** self-checks on OrangeFox, TWRP and PBRP (UI, touch, decrypt,
  backups, haptics, MTP/ADB, six panels).
- **ADB sideload verified on OrangeFox 12.1** (test zip installed). TWRP/PBRP use the
  same fixed `init.recovery.usb.rc` in the final source tree.
- `/cache` does not exist on this device; Dalvik/ART is the only cache wipe.

## Not usable on this phone

- **OrangeFox 14.1 / 16.0**: both bootloop in recovery userspace; the full images also
  exceed the ~55.5 MB `vendor_boot` limit. Do not flash them.
- **TWRP 14.1 source**: builds, but its userspace does not reach the UI on this A16
  vendor.

## Flash

```powershell
fastboot flash vendor_boot_a <image>.img
```

Then enter recovery from the **bootloader menu**: **Volume Down → Recovery mode → Power**.
`fastboot reboot recovery` boots Android on this unit. Keep the stock `vendor_boot` for
revert.

## GSI

The LineageOS 23.2 microG Android 16 GSI from the previous release remains the tested
system image. The modem `md1img_a` unlock is built/audited but **not network-tested**
(no SIM); see `docs/07-modem-unlock.md`.
